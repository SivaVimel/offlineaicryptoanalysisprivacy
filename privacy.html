Offline AI Crypto AnalysisPrivacy Policy

Privacy, plainly

No account. No ads. No analytics. No servers of ours. Your watchlist, holdings and alerts are stored only on your device, and the AI runs there too.

In one line: we collect nothing. Your device fetches public market data from Yahoo Finance directly, and everything else happens on the device.

What stays on your device

  • Your watchlist, holdings (quantities and the costs you enter), price alerts and saved screens.
  • Settings: appearance, display currency, comparison coin, refresh interval, AI preferences.
  • Cached market data, charts and AI summaries, so the app opens quickly and works with the last data it had.

This data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by iOS file encryption, and included in your own device backups. You can export it, restore it, or erase it at any time in Settings.

What your device requests from the internet

RequestSent toWhat it contains
Market list, charts, sparklines, exchange ratesYahoo FinanceCoin symbols and time ranges. Nothing about you or your holdings.
Search and headlinesYahoo FinanceThe search text you type, or a coin name.
Opening a headlineThe publisher, in SafariWhat any web page request contains.

These requests go directly from your device; they do not pass through any server of ours. They carry no cookies and no identifier from us. Like any internet request they carry your IP address, which Yahoo's own privacy policy governs. Your holdings, quantities, costs, alerts and questions are never sent.

The AI

The AI analyst is Meta's Llama 3.2 1B model, bundled inside the app and run on your device through llama.cpp. Your questions, and the figures it phrases, are processed on the device and are not sent anywhere. Generation runs only while you are looking at it.

Optionally, you can point the app at an AI endpoint you run or choose yourself — for example a model server on your own network — in Settings → AI engine → Custom endpoint. Only then, the figures for the coin or market on screen and your question are sent to that endpoint, at the address and with the credentials you enter. It is off unless you set it up, and your holdings are never sent. The app offers no third-party cloud AI services.

Notifications and Face ID

Price-alert notifications are created on your device; no push server is involved. If you turn on app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is handled entirely by iOS — the app never receives biometric data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
